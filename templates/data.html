LIMITATIONS

What this project cannot claim

  • It cannot estimate unreported crime from registered-case totals.
  • It does not identify offenders or predict guilt.
  • Demo incidents are not official police records.
  • URL checks do not contact threat-intelligence services or visit the URL.
  • State totals should not be compared without considering population and reporting differences.
